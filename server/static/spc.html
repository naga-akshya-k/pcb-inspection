PROCESS IN STATISTICAL CONTROL
Red Line = Upper Limit (UCL)

The Danger Threshold (+3σ). If a green dot crosses above this line, the SMT machine is drifting out of control and requires immediate feeder nozzle maintenance.

Yellow Line = Center Line (CL)

Process Mean Baseline. The historical average defect rate across all runs. A healthy line keeps this baseline as close to 0.00 as possible.

Purple Line = Lower Limit (LCL)

The Zero-Defect Floor (0.00). Represents 100% first-pass yield with zero defects found on the board.

Green Dots = Your Live Boards

Each dot is one inspected PCB. As long as all green dots remain under the Red UCL line, the manufacturing process is certified Statistically In-Control.
